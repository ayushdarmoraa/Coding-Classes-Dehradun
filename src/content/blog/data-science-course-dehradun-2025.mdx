## Published course details

The [Data Science & AI course page](/courses/data-science) lists a six-month duration, a ₹30,000 package rate, a separate ₹6,000/month rate option, and a curriculum covering:

- Python, NumPy, and Pandas
- Data cleaning and visualization
- Statistics and probability basics
- Machine-learning algorithms, including regression, trees, and clustering
- Model evaluation and tuning
- An introduction to generative AI and LLM tooling

The course record lists these learning outcomes: analyzing datasets and building machine-learning models, creating notebooks and reports, and completing a mini capstone project. These are curriculum descriptions, not job or salary guarantees.
