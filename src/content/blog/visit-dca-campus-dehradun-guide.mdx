# Visit Doon Coding Academy (DCA) Campus (Dehradun): A Practical Guide

If you’re planning to visit **Doon Coding Academy (DCA)’s campus in Dehradun**, here’s everything you need to know: address, transport options, timings, and what to expect when you arrive.

## Address & Timings
- **Address:** Near DR School, Herbertpur (Dehradun district), PIN 248142  
- **Timings:** Monday–Saturday, 9:30 AM – 6:30 PM  
- Closed on Sundays and public holidays.  

👉 [View on Google Maps](https://maps.google.com/?q=Near+DR+School+Herbertpur+Dehradun)

## How to Reach
- **By bus:** Frequent buses from ISBT Dehradun towards Herbertpur/Dakpathar.  
- **From Dehradun Railway Station:** Take a bus or cab towards Herbertpur.  
- **By car/auto:** Ample parking nearby. Located just off Ambala–Dehradun Rd (NH 7).  

## What to Bring on Your Visit
- Updated **resume** (if you want career counseling).  
- **Portfolio/project work** if applying for advanced courses.  
- List of questions about course curriculum, placement, or fees.  

## Mini-Campus Tour
During your visit you can explore:  
- Classrooms & labs equipped for project-based learning  
- Student lounge and mentor support area  
- Placement cell and career guidance desk

## FAQs

**Do I need to book an appointment to visit?**  
Walk-ins are fine, but booking ensures a mentor is available to guide you.  

**Can I attend a trial class?**  
Yes, trial sessions are available — ask our team during booking.  

**Is the campus open on Saturdays?**  
Yes, 9:30 AM – 6:30 PM. Closed Sundays.
