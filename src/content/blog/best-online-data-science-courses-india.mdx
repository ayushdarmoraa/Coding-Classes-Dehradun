# How to Evaluate Online Data Science Courses in India

Course names alone do not tell you what is taught or how a program is delivered. Use these questions to compare published course details and confirm anything that is not stated.

## What to compare

- **Curriculum:** Check whether the listed topics match your learning goals.
- **Duration and fees:** Compare the published figures and ask whether they are current.
- **Delivery:** Confirm whether the course is currently available online, how sessions work, and what support is included.
- **Practice:** Ask what assignments or projects are included; do not assume project reviews or portfolio support.
- **Career support:** Ask the provider what, if any, services are currently offered. Course completion does not guarantee employment.

## A practical self-study roadmap

Use this sequence as a flexible outline and adjust it to your background and available time:

1. Learn Python basics and practice reading, cleaning, and summarizing tabular data.
2. Add SQL fundamentals and exploratory data analysis, including charts that answer specific questions.
3. Study the statistics and probability needed to interpret results, then try a few machine-learning methods.
4. Evaluate a simple baseline, document errors and limitations, and explain what the results do and do not show.
5. Present the work in a clear notebook or report with reproducible steps and links to the data source.

## Portfolio project ideas

- Analyze an openly licensed public dataset and explain the questions, cleaning steps, and limitations.
- Build a small sales or service dashboard with clear labels and a short written summary.
- Compare a simple forecasting or classification model with a baseline, including an error analysis.
- Create a reproducible notebook that explores a public dataset and documents its source and permitted use.

Do not include personal, confidential, or restricted data in a public portfolio. Cite datasets and follow their license terms.

## Questions to ask before enrolling

1. Is this course available in the mode I need right now?
2. What is the current timetable and what happens if I miss a session?
3. Which practical exercises or projects are included?
4. What feedback or learner support is provided, if any?
5. Are any career services available, and what do they include?

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I study data science without a master's degree?

A master's degree is not a prerequisite for learning the fundamentals. Check the requirements for each role or program you are considering.

### What math should I learn for data science?

Start with descriptive statistics, probability, and the algebra used in the material you are studying. Add more depth as your goals and projects require it.

### How long does it take to learn data science?

There is no fixed timeline. It depends on your starting point, available study time, and the topics you want to cover; use a roadmap as a guide rather than a completion promise.
