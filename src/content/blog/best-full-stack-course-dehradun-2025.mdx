# Full-Stack Development Course in Dehradun

Compare course curricula, duration, fees, prerequisites, and delivery availability before choosing a program. This guide summarizes the current published Full-Stack course record at Doon Coding Academy (DCA); confirm current batch information with the academy.

## Published curriculum

The six-month Full-Stack Development curriculum lists:

- HTML, CSS, and Tailwind fundamentals
- Modern JavaScript and TypeScript basics
- React, hooks, routing, and state management
- Node.js, Express APIs, and MongoDB
- Authentication, security, and validation
- Gen AI APIs, including prompting and simple RAG
- Project work, Git/GitHub, and deployment with Vercel

The course record lists outcomes including building full-stack apps, deploying production projects, and a portfolio with two to three projects. These describe the course's intended learning outcomes and are not job, interview, or placement guarantees.
The published pricing lists a ₹25,000 package rate and a separate ₹5,000/month rate option.

## What to compare before enrolling

- Check that the curriculum matches the topics you want to study.
- Review the prerequisites, duration, and published outcomes.
- Confirm schedule, delivery format, and payment details directly with the provider.
- Ask whether any support or services you need are included. Do not assume recordings, mentoring, project feedback, internships, or placement assistance are provided.
